# NEON//ZERO — Character Prompts

## Ren Kurogami

18-year-old lean athletic Japanese anime male protagonist. Messy black hair, blue-gray eyes, sharp youthful face. Wearing a black futuristic courier jacket with glowing cyan lines, dark tactical clothing, futuristic boots, and a small ZERO symbol. Serious and determined expression. High-budget Japanese sci-fi anime style, cinematic lighting, detailed character design.

## Mira

17-year-old slim athletic female character. Long silver-white hair, icy blue eyes, calm intelligent expression. Wearing a black and white futuristic combat suit with cyan glowing details and carrying a futuristic energy pistol. High-budget Japanese sci-fi anime style, cinematic lighting, detailed character design.

## Kai

19-year-old athletic male rival. Messy dark crimson-red hair, amber eyes, sharp confident expression. Wearing a black futuristic combat jacket with crimson technological accents and carrying an advanced futuristic energy weapon. High-budget Japanese sci-fi anime style, cinematic lighting.

## AETHER

Advanced humanoid artificial intelligence with pale silver-white synthetic skin, symmetrical elegant face, glowing icy-blue eyes, emotionless expression, silver-white mechanical hair, and white-silver futuristic armor with black mechanical sections. Cyan energy lines run across the body. A giant circular mechanical halo surrounds AETHER. The AETHER Core glows behind it. Elegant, powerful, and mysterious futuristic design.

## Character Consistency

All characters must maintain the same face, hairstyle, eye color, clothing, body proportions, and overall visual identity throughout NEON//ZERO.

Style: High-budget Japanese sci-fi anime, cinematic 2D/3D animation, detailed line art, dramatic lighting, neon cyan and crimson accents, futuristic cyberpunk environments.
