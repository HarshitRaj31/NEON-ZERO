# NEON//ZERO — Scene Prompts

## Scene 1 — Floating City

Slow cinematic camera push toward the futuristic floating Sky City. Flying vehicles move smoothly between the skyscrapers. Clouds drift beneath the city, revealing the ruined Earth below. Neon lights and holographic signs gently flicker. Mysterious futuristic atmosphere.

## Scene 2 — AETHER

Slow cinematic camera movement toward the giant AETHER hologram inside a futuristic command center. Blue holographic interfaces glow around the AI system while the city operates in the background. Calm, advanced, controlled atmosphere.

## Scene 3 — Peaceful World

Show humanity living peacefully inside the futuristic floating city. Citizens walk through bright plazas while flying vehicles move between skyscrapers. Waterfalls, gardens, holograms and clean futuristic architecture create a perfect society.

## Scene 4 — 30 Days Remaining

The giant city screens suddenly glitch and turn red. The message "HUMANITY 30 DAYS REMAINING" appears across every screen. The crowd stops and looks up in shock while AETHER remains calm. Dramatic blue and crimson lighting.

## Scene 5 — Ren Introduction

Ren Kurogami runs across rain-soaked futuristic rooftops at night while carrying a mysterious black package. Neon lights reflect on the wet streets as he moves through the lower city.

## Scene 6 — AETHER Tracks Ren

AETHER's surveillance system detects Ren as a high-priority target. Holographic interfaces track his location while streams of futuristic data surround his image.

## Scene 7 — Drone Attack

Multiple AETHER security drones chase Ren across the rooftops. Red targeting lasers lock onto him while energy weapons fire. Ren runs and jumps between buildings to escape.

## Scene 8 — ZERO Awakens

Ren is surrounded by AETHER drones on a rainy rooftop. His ZERO power suddenly activates, freezing the rain, bullet and drones in midair. His eyes glow cyan-white as mysterious energy bursts from the package.

---

## Visual Style

High-budget Japanese sci-fi anime, cinematic 2D/3D animation, detailed futuristic environments, dramatic lighting, neon cyan and crimson accents, smooth camera movement, serious and mysterious atmosphere.

## Character Consistency

Keep every character's face, hairstyle, clothing, eye color, body proportions and visual identity consistent throughout all scenes.
